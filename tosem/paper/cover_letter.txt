Dear Editors of ACM Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology,

We are pleased to submit the revised version of our manuscript entitled
"TyFlow: A Type-Aware Approach to Neural Code Models" (manuscript
TOSEM-2026-0076), prepared in response to the Major Revision decision of
June 16, 2026.

All comments from the editor and the three reviewers have been addressed
with new experiments, new analyses, and targeted revisions to the paper.
The main additions are a failure taxonomy over all Java test tasks
(App. C), two added Java benchmarks that enlarge the evaluation to 186
tasks together with a per-language significance analysis (App. B, App. D),
a comparison with three larger decoder-only models (Table 3), a comparison
with four reliability-oriented generation methods (Table 5), runtime and
pruning measurements of the constrained decoder (RQ2), and a new
Limitations subsection (Sec. 6.3). A point-by-point response to every
comment accompanies this submission.

Files provided with this submission:

1. manuscript.pdf - the revised manuscript (clean version, 45 pages).

2. manuscript_marked.pdf - the same revised manuscript with all changes
   marked: additions shown in blue, deletions struck through in red.

3. revision_response_letter.pdf - the response letter: each reviewer
   comment quoted in full, followed by our detailed response, a summary
   of the changes made, and the corresponding passages quoted from the
   revised manuscript.

This is an original submission and is not an extended version of any
previously published conference paper. The manuscript has not been
published in any peer-reviewed venue and is not under consideration by
any other journal. A preprint version is available on arXiv. The authors
declare no conflicts of interest.

Thank you for considering our revised submission. We look forward to the
reviewers' feedback.
